Rarity

Every round holds 111 beings, split across eight tiers. Only ten Gods will ever exist — one per sector.

Rewards

Sector Share

Higher tiers — Epic and above — hold a share of the revenue flowing through their sector. The rarer the being, the deeper the channel.

The Witnesses

Each round, a set of Commons and Uncommons is chosen as Witnesses. They saw what happened, so they are paid for it. No being is worthless here.

The Pilgrim Bonus

Hold beings from several sectors and you walk the realm rather than camp in it. Pilgrims earn an increasing bonus with every new sector they hold.

Nothing here is a promise of profit. REALM is an art and community project. Rewards depend on activity that may not happen.

    FAQ

    How many NFTs are there in total?

    1,110. Ten rounds of 111, one sector per round.

    How many can I mint?

    Three per wallet, per round.

    What chain is REALM on?

    Solana. You'll need a Solana wallet such as Phantom, Solflare or Backpack, and some SOL for the mint and network fees.

    Do I know which tier I'm getting?

    No. Every being stays sealed until the round finishes and the reveal happens. That keeps the rare tiers fair.

    Are the common ones worthless?

    No. Commons and Uncommons can be chosen as Witnesses each round, and they carry the story of their sector.

    What happens after round 10?

    The map is complete, the Source opens, and the collection closes forever. No round 11.
